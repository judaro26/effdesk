Everything you need to go from sign-up to your first answered ticket, in about ten minutes.

Tip: You can follow this guide on your phone too. Every step works on small screens.

Before you start

Make sure you have your company name, a work email address and the number of agents you plan to invite. Pick a short workspace address: it becomes your help center web address.

  1. Open the sign-up page.
  2. Enter your details and confirm your email.
  3. Choose a plan, or stay on the free plan.

Invite your teammates

Open Manage, then People, and choose Invite. Each role can do different things:

RoleCan replyCan change settings
OwnerYesYes
AgentYesNo
Light agentInternal notes onlyNo

Heads up: invitations expire after seven days.
