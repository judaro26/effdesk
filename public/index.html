Questions, answered.

What counts as an agent?

Anyone who answers customers. On Team and above, light agents who only read tickets and leave internal notes do not use a seat. Customers never count.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Two agents, email tickets, macros and a basic help center, with no card needed and no time limit.

Can I change or cancel later?

Yes. You can change plan or cancel from your account page. Paid plans are billed per agent, yearly at the lowest price or monthly.

Who handles payment and tax?

Our payment partner, Paddle, takes payment and handles sales tax and invoices. Card details never touch our servers.

What does “Soon” mean?

Those features are planned but not released. We list them so you can see where each plan is going, and we will not charge you for a feature before it works.

Can we use our own address for the help center?

Every workspace gets its own address, like yourname.effdesk.com. Using a domain of your own is on our list.

Is there an API?

Yes, on the Suite plan: tickets, replies and custom fields over REST, plus signed webhooks. The developer guide has examples you can paste.
